## Wolf (C. l. arabs) -- OPENED en.wikipedia 'Arabian wolf' + ar.wikipedia 'ذئب عربي'
- shoulder 64-66 cm (25-26 in) [en.wp citing UKWCT leaflet]; ar.wp 'about 66 cm'
- weight: mean 20 kg (45 lb) [en.wp citing Lopez 2004 p18]; en.wp 'Wolf': Indian+Arabian 25 kg avg (Lopez 1978 p19);
  ar.wp '25 kg (45 lb)' (inconsistent conversion). search summaries: 18 kg (40 lb) others.
- skull ~20 cm (Walker 2008) vs northern 230-280 mm (Mech 1981)
- ears proportionally larger than other subspecies (Mills 1945)
- coat: short thin, greyish beige (Pocock 1935); 'mixture of black and slightly buffy grey' (Pocock 1935 SE Arabia)
- summer coat short, BUT back hair stays long in summer (Harrington & Paquet 1982 p474, ar.wp); winter coat long but not
  as long as northern
- melanistic wolves recorded in SA western highlands (Zafar-ul Islam 2019)
- pads of 3rd & 4th toes occasionally fused (Ferguson & Menache 2002) [en]; ar.wp: middle two toes fused (Macdonald 1992)
- packs of 2-4; mostly at night
- no length/tail/by-sex figures found for arabs. Proxies (opened en.wp):
  Indian wolf: shoulder 57-72, male 19-25 kg, female 17-22 kg, nose-to-tail-tip 103-145 cm (CZA studbook)
  Wolf (general, Heptner&Naumov): length 105-160, shoulder 80-85, tail 29-50 cm, ears 90-110 mm high, hind foot 220-250;
    females 5-10 lb lighter; females narrower muzzle, thinner neck, slightly shorter legs (Lopez 1978)
  search summary only: Egypt wolf 114-140 total, tail 31-45 cm (unverified page)
- wolf vs jackal (en.wp, Heptner): wolf larger, broader snout, shorter ears(than jackal), shorter torso, longer tail,
  longer legs; jackal: elongated torso, less-prominent forehead, narrower more pointed muzzle, shorter legs+tail.
  Golden jackal: body 71-85 m / 69-73 f cm, 6-14 kg; Syria/region jackals 60-90 body, tail 20-30, head 15-18 cm.
- wolf (Heptner): deep descending rib cage, sloping back, heavily muscled neck, legs moderately longer than other canids
- Eurasian wolf colour (Heptner p168): muzzle pale ochreous grey; lips cheeks chin throat white; black-tipped dorsal
  stripe, black tips on shoulders, upper chest, rump; inner limbs, belly, groin white.
- Indian wolf (Mivart 1890): dark V-shaped patch around shoulders; limbs paler; underparts white.

## Dogs
- Canaan dog (en.wp OPENED; FCI-ish standard by Menzel 1966): male 50-60 cm, female 45-50 cm, male 18-25 kg; aka
  'Bedouin Sheep dog'; square build, wedge head, erect low-set broad ears rounded tips; outer coat short-medium dense
  harsh; undercoat by season; colours black..cream, browns/reds, small white marks or white with patches; tail curled
  over back when excited; loin short. Bedouin dogs mostly mixed today (en.wp).
- Saudi practice (SEARCH SUMMARY only): Panthera [149] AlUla farmers use 'security dogs', enclosures, herdsmen;
  Cunningham/Wronski Asir: 'dogs used to protect free roaming goat herds'. NO breed named in any source found.
  Kurdish/Kangal imports: not found. Commercial SA page: German shepherd 'black jack' sold as guard dogs 2000-5000 SAR
  (mstaml, search only, weak).
- Kangal (en.wp OPENED, FCI): male 70-80 cm 48-60 kg; female 63-75 cm 40-50 kg; spiked wolf collar common in Turkey.
  LGD article: 'wolf collar' commonly used.
- Saluki (en.wp OPENED): 58-71 cm (23-28 in), 16-32 kg (35-70 lb) (infobox 18-27 kg); 'desert/Hijazi saluki' type;
  quarry gazelle hare fox jackal; Guinness 1996 68.8 km/h.

## Gait
- en.wp 'Canine gait' OPENED ({{Refimprove}}): walk = 3 feet support, one foot at a time in regular sequence; trot =
  2-beat diagonal; pace = lateral pairs; canter 3-beat; gallop single/double suspension; footfall it gives RF,LF,RH,LH.
- search summary: walk order RH, RF, LH, LF (lateral sequence); rotary gallop in dogs at speed (Hildebrand 1977 via
  Biancardi & Minetti 2012), transverse at lower speed.
- speeds: Mech 1994 (USGS, search summary) arctic wolves travel 8.7 km/h, 10.0 homeward; chase 56-64 km/h popular
  sources only. Saluki 68.8 km/h (Guinness via en.wp).
