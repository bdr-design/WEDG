# T7: the rifle's design, decided by the director (the owner, 13 Nov 2026: «انت صمم لي الكلاشنكوف»)

The owner sends no photos: the look is the director's decision, below. It replaces "awaits the owner's photos" in the
brief. Build exactly this; where a detail's size has no source, take it from a scaled reference drawing and mark it.

## The rifle

The AKM pattern, as the wooden «كلاش روسي» is known in the Gulf, kept by a herder who looks after it: clean, used,
not new and not battered.

- Receiver: stamped steel (the AKM's, not the milled AK-47's): the small dimple over the magazine well on each side,
  the trunnion rivets front and rear, the ribbed stamped dust cover, the selector lever on the right with its stop
  notches, the charging handle on the right, the magazine catch and trigger guard.
- Barrel and front end: the gas block with the gas tube above the barrel, the front sight tower with its two ears
  and post, the AKM's slanted muzzle compensator, the cleaning rod under the barrel, the bayonet lug.
- Sights: the rear tangent leaf (graduated, its slider), the front post.
- Wood: laminated birch, the AKM's shapes: the fixed stock (straight comb, steel butt plate), the lower handguard
  with its palm swells on both sides, the upper handguard over the gas tube. Colour: a warm red-brown shellac, the
  amber-orange of Soviet laminate darkened by handling, with the laminate's fine layer lines along the stock's length
  and a soft sheen (roughness about 0.45), darker and more matt where hands hold it (the grip area of the handguard,
  the stock's wrist).
- Pistol grip: the AKM's bakelite-type grip, a dark red-brown, slightly mottled, smoother and glossier than the wood.
- Magazine: the 30-round stamped steel one with its ribs and the curve, the same black as the receiver.
- Metal finish: black oxide, a deep satin black (not grey, not glossy), with honest wear that every used AK has:
  silvered edges on the muzzle, the front sight ears, the charging handle, the magazine's lower front edge and its
  ribs, the corners of the dust cover; and the selector lever's arc scratched bright into the receiver's right side.
  No rust, no dents.
- Sling: a plain canvas web sling in khaki-olive with its steel hooks, from the front swivel (left of the gas block)
  to the stock's swivel (left side), as a separate part so it can be shown slung or left off.
- No maker's marks, arsenal stamps, serial numbers or writing of any kind.
