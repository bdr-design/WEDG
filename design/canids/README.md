## Known gaps, in plain words

- No rig or movement yet: every pose is a still model.
- The toes are painted, not modelled; the closed mouth has no teeth (the barking mouth has its canines).
- The stray's thinness is painted (ribs, hip bones); its outline is the guard dog's.
