# The birds of the hunt: pictures

**Awaiting the owner's approval of the look.** Built by `tool/bird3d` (its README has the sources, the measurements
and the weight on the phone); the models are in `assets_src/birds/`.

- `saker_hooded_*`, `saker_alert_*`, `saker_flight_*`: the saker, a first-year female (فرخ), hooded on her perch,
  unhooded and alert, and cast off. `birds_fist.jpg`: hooded on the falconer's left fist (الدس).
- `houbara_*`: the houbara (الحبارى), a male, alert and in flight.
- `curlew_*`: the stone-curlew (الكروان), alert and in flight.
- `birds_scale.jpg`: all of them beside the player's figure, with a 2 m ruler.

Side pictures carry a centimetre ruler; flight pictures are seen from the side, straight from below and from above.

## Known gaps, in plain words

- No rig or movement yet: every pose is a still model. It comes after the look is approved.
- The man's fingers cannot curl yet, so on the fist his hand is hidden inside the cuff; the leash is not drawn on the
  fist.
- A faint dark line where the saker's face meets the cere, seen only in the close head picture.
- The houbara's crest is a small fan of flat feathers and still reads as a small comb, simpler than the real loose
  plumes.
- On the fist a few toe and talon vertices sit up to about 3 mm inside the padded cuff (by the reviewer's contact
  test), where the talons run down its steep front; the falcon's tail tip passes behind the cuff.
- The far pictures of the flight feathers show fine stripes (the barring is finer than the texture can hold at a
  distance).
- Wing areas, the houbara's and stone-curlew's leg and wing lengths, and the gear's sizes beyond the perch's top are
  drawn in proportion: no measured figure was found (marked as game choices in the tool's README).
