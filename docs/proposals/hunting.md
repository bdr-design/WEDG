## 1. What the owner asked (13 Nov 2026)

«ابي بندقية كلاش، نبي ندخل موضوع الصيد»: a Kalashnikov in the game, and hunting as a
subject. The rifle model is built in a separate task (T7); this file is about what

## 2. The real rules in short

| Question | What is real today |
|---|---|
| Season | 1 Sep 2026 – 31 Jan 2027, autumn and winter halves, 30 permitted native species, by day only (from an hour after sunrise to an hour before sunset) [127][128][135][140] |
| Licence | A permit per hunter from NCW's «فطري» platform, through النفاذ الوطني; for holders of licensed air/gas rifles and falconers registered with نادي الصقور; falcons need a passport or ownership certificate [128][131] |
| Lawful means | Air and gas rifles without gunpowder («الساكتون»), registered falcons, hunting dogs (السلق) [131][132][135][142] |
| Banned means | Shotguns (الشوزن) and spread shot, nets, traps, calling and luring devices, hunting from a moving vehicle, **any powder firearm** [127][130][136][137] |
| Banned places | Cities, villages, **farms**, rest houses, near vital sites, protected areas and royal reserves, giga-projects, the coast to 20 km inland [127][134][135] |
| Typical game | Doves (اليمام), sandgrouse (القطا), quail, partridges with air rifles; stone-curlew (الكروان) with falcons; houbara (الحبارى) with falcons in licensed fenced estates (المحازات); hares, jerboas and the dhub with dogs in those estates [140][142] |
| Never | The Arabian wolf, leopard, oryx, gazelles, ibex, hyena, all birds of prey, the European turtle dove (القميري) [134][139][141] |
| Fines | No permit 10,000; banned place or time 5,000; firearm 80,000; shotgun, nets, moving vehicle 100,000; per animal: wolf 80,000, hare 18,000, dhub 3,000 (SAR) [136][139] |
| Selling game | Showing or selling hunted animals without a licence is an offence [166] |
| The Kalashnikov | An automatic rifle is a war weapon (سلاح حربي): owning, carrying, buying or selling one is reported as up to 15 years and SAR 150,000 [145]. Hunting with it is also the firearm offence (SAR 80,000 [136], and up to a year and SAR 5,000 under the Weapons Law [137]). Whether a semi-automatic AK can be licensed: **not found** |
| Wolves at the herd | The wolf is endangered and protected (80,000 per wolf). Herders' lawful defence is guard dogs, strong pens and herdsmen [149]. No rule found that allows shooting a wolf that attacks a herd |

**The plain answer to the owner's question:** hunting with a Kalashnikov is not legal in
the Kingdom, and owning an automatic one is a serious crime. Lawful hunting is with an air
rifle, a falcon or a saluki, in season, with a فطري permit, far from farms and towns.

## 3. What every option keeps

- **Realism first:** the law above applies as it is; every number is sourced or marked.
- **Money is the only progression (GDD 8):** hunting gives no levels or points. Since
  selling game is unlawful [166], hunting is a **cost and a pastime**, not an income,
  unless it goes through falcons, which are really bought, sold and entered for prizes.
- **The map is the centre (GDD 9):** no hunting screen. The player drives the Land
  Cruiser out of the farm onto the real ground; the means of hunting appear as icon
  buttons at the screen's edge while the car is stopped in the open, as the car's own
  buttons do (GDD 9.1). Nothing written floats; a card opens only when tapped.
- **The in-game phone:** permits, licences and the falcon's passport live in phone apps.

## 4. The options

### A. المقناص with a falcon (الصقر)

- **What the player does:**
  1. Buys a falcon: at the breeders' auction at Malham (August), or a locally trapped bird
     at the National Falcon Centre's auction (1 Oct – 30 Nov) [157][158].
  2. Registers it (the falcon's passport) and himself with the Falcons Club, then takes a
     season permit in the phone's فطري app.
  3. Trains it in September and early October (الدعو) [155]: a short daily care task at
     the farm, like the herd's care.
  4. In season, drives out with it in the Land Cruiser, stops in open land outside the
     banned areas, looks for tracks, and slips the falcon at a stone-curlew. Houbara
     only in a licensed hunting estate (محازة), which is a place on the map with a pin.
- **What it earns or costs:** costs the falcon (farm-bred sakers about SAR 19,000 [158];
  top birds over a million [158]), the permit (fee **not found**: game choice), feed and
  the trip's fuel and time. Earns nothing directly. A trained falcon is worth more on
  resale (**game choice**: how much training adds).
- **Real:** the season, the permit, the passport, the auctions and their months, the
  quarry by means, the trip in 4x4s with two or three men a car and a camp [155][156].
- **Game choice:** the falcon's skill and how training raises it; the chance a slip
  succeeds; fee amounts; how a hunt is shown.
- **Weight on the phone (estimate):** one skinned falcon (about 2–3k triangles, 1 draw,
  no shadow under the 30 cm rule unless it is on the fist) and one or two skinned prey
  birds (about 2k each). The hunt is away from the herd, so it shares the frame with the
  terrain and the car only: about +4 draws and +8k triangles over the drive view.

### B. Air-rifle hunting (الساكتون) in season

- **What the player does:** owns a licensed air rifle (licence in the phone), takes a فطري
  permit, drives out, stops in the open and shoots doves, sandgrouse or quail, one bird a
  shot, within the permit's numbers and by day.
- **What it earns or costs:** costs the rifle (price **not researched**: game choice), the
  licence and the permit. Earns nothing (game cannot be sold).
- **Real:** air/gas rifles without powder are the only lawful guns [131][132]; one bird a
  shot [127]; species by season [140]. An air rifle has no muzzle flash, so the effect is a
  sound and a puff of dust where the pellet lands.
- **Game choice:** the bag limit per day (the real number was **not found**), the aiming
  control, the chance of a hit.
- **Weight on the phone (estimate):** a small flock of birds as one instanced draw (a few
  hundred triangles each), the rifle model from T7 (to be measured there), a dust puff as
  one particle draw. About +3 draws and +10k triangles.

### C. The Kalashnikov (الكلاش)

The rifle is the owner's ask, and the law around it is real. Four ways to place it:

- **C1. Not in the game.** The most realistic for a lawful camel owner. Loses what the
  owner asked for.
- **C2. Allowed, with its risk (like cheating in GDD 7.1).** The player can buy one
  (from whom, and the price, are **game choices**: there is no lawful seller). It is kept
  at the farm. It is never a lawful hunting tool:
  - Hunting with it: the firearm fine SAR 80,000 [136] plus the Weapons Law [137], and
    the hunting fines of what was shot (wolf 80,000 each [139]).
  - Being found with it (a chance when the security forces stop the car in the open, or at
    the farm: **game choice**): the weapon is taken and the war-weapon penalty applies
    (up to 15 years and SAR 150,000 [145]). How a prison term plays in a game that keeps
    running is the owner's decision (section 7, question 6).
  - Firing it at a wedding or celebration is a crime warranting arrest [147].
- **C3. On the wall only.** Kept in the بيت الشعر or the room as a thing to look at; it
  is never fired. Owning it is still the same offence if it is automatic, so C3 is C2 with
  no use.
- **C4. A licensed rifle instead.** If the owner wants a rifle the player may lawfully
  own, it has to be an individual firearm licensed through Absher (Saudi, 22 or older,
  clean record, toxicology test [146]). Even licensed, it may not be used to hunt [137].
  Whether an AK-pattern semi-automatic can be such a rifle was **not found**.

**Weight on the phone (estimate):** the model is T7's; it adds nothing when it is not in
view. If it is fired: a muzzle flash as one short-lived quad and a sound.

### D. The herd's safety: wolves and stray dogs (الذئب والكلاب الضالة)

- **What the player does:** nothing new to tap. Losses of calves (حيران) at night become
  possible when pens are weak, there is no herdsman, or the herd sleeps out at a grazing
  camp. The defences are the real ones: a strong pen, a herdsman, a guard dog (a new
  thing to buy) [149].
- **What it earns or costs:** a guard dog's price and feed (**game choice**), and the
  calves that are lost without one.
- **Real:** wolves enter pens and kill livestock; herders defend with dogs, pens and
  herdsmen; the wolf is protected and killing one is SAR 80,000 [139][148][149]. Stray
  dog attacks are real in Qassim and elsewhere [151].
- **Game choice:** how often a predator comes; that it takes calves rather than adult
  camels (wolves' real attacks on camels were **not found**: the sources speak of sheep
  and goats).
- **Weight on the phone (estimate):** a wolf or a dog at night near the farm is one
  skinned animal (about 3–4k triangles) inside the 16-skinned budget, shown only in an
  event. Close to zero otherwise.

### E. Falcons as a second contest line (later, if ever)

The falconry festival at Malham has a lure race (الملواح) and a beauty contest (مزاين
الصقور), with prizes reported above SAR 38 million [157], and falcons are a market of
their own [158]. A falcon line could mirror the camel line: buy, train, enter, sell. It
is the only form of hunting that earns money under the real rules, but it is a second
game beside the camels, and it is a scope decision for the owner.

### F. Salukis (السلق)

Hunting hares, jerboas or the dhub with salukis happens in the dog zones of licensed
fenced estates [142]. It is real but narrow; a saluki is one more skinned animal
(about 4k triangles). It could ride with A as an estate visit.

## 5. Where it meets the calendar

The season (1 Sep – 31 Jan) lies over the الوسم rains (from 16 Oct), the falcon auctions
(Oct–Nov), the falconry festival (Nov, or Dec–Jan as in 2025), the camel festival (Dec –
early Jan) and mating (from Nov) (GDD 4.4). A trip costs days that the herd and the
festival also want: the trade-off is the player's, and it is real.

## 6. What it must not become

- Not a shooter: no crosshair game for points, no blood, no kill counts written on screen.
- Not a money farm: game is not sold.
- Not a war on wolves: the wolf is protected; there is no reward for killing one.
- Not near the farm: hunting at or around the عزبة is illegal (farms are a banned place),
  so the farm view never becomes a hunting ground.
- Not a new permanent overlay: no hunting bar or menu over the map.

## 7. Questions for the owner (recommendation first)

1. **The Kalashnikov.** *Recommended:* C2, allowed with its real risk as cheating is in 7.1,
   never a lawful hunting tool, with C1 as the realist's alternative.
2. **Which hunting comes first.** *Recommended:* A (المقناص with a falcon), then B (air rifle).
3. **Falcon contests (E).** *Recommended:* not now; keep the camels at the centre.
4. **Predators at the herd (D).** *Recommended:* yes, with the real defences (pen, herdsman,
   guard dog), and shooting a wolf is an offence.
5. **Real names in the phone** (فطري, أبشر, نادي الصقور). *Recommended:* use the real names as
   text, without their logos, as the game already names the real festivals.
6. **A prison term** (the war-weapon penalty). *Recommended:* the owner chooses between a
   heavy fine with the weapon taken and a ban from the festivals (as in 7.1), or a pause
   in which the herdsman runs the farm.
