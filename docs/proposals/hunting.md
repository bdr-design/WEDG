## 0. Read these official pages before anything here is adopted

No official page could be opened from the session that wrote this, so every fact rests on

## 2. The real rules in short

| Question | What is real today |
|---|---|
| Season | 1 Sep 2026 – 31 Jan 2027, an autumn half (to 30 Nov) and a winter half, 30 permitted native species [127][128][167]. By day only, from an hour after sunrise to an hour before sunset (one article [135]) |
| Licence | A permit per hunter from NCW's «فطري» platform, through النفاذ الوطني, for holders of licensed air/gas rifles and for falconers registered with نادي الصقور; a falcon needs a passport or ownership certificate [128][131]. Minimum age 21 (one source [135]) |
| Lawful means in open land | Air and gas rifles without gunpowder («الساكتون»), and registered falcons [131][132] |
| Salukis (السلق) | Named in the regulation as a traditional means [135], but confirmed only in the dog zones of licensed fenced estates (المحازات) [142]. No permit route for open land was found (uncertain) |
| Banned means | Shotguns (الشوزن) and spread shot, nets, traps, calling and luring devices, hunting from a moving vehicle, **any powder firearm** [127][130][136][137] |
| Banned places | Cities, villages, **farms**, rest houses, near vital sites, protected areas and royal reserves, the giga-projects, the coast to 20 km inland [127][134][135] |
| Typical game | Doves (اليمام), sandgrouse (القطا), quail and partridges with air rifles; stone-curlew (الكروان) with falcons [140]. Inside licensed estates: houbara (الحبارى) with falcons, and hares, jerboas and the dhub with dogs [142] |
| Never, in the wild | The Arabian wolf, leopard, oryx, gazelles, ibex, hyena, all birds of prey, the European turtle dove (القميري) [134][139][141]. Oryx, gazelles and ibex are hunted in the estates' professional zones, presumably bred stock (uncertain [142]); **this proposal does not offer them as quarry** |
| Fines | No permit 10,000; banned place or time 5,000; firearm 80,000; shotgun, nets or moving vehicle 100,000. Per animal: wolf 80,000, hare 18,000, dhub 3,000 (SAR) [136][139] |
| Selling game | Showing or selling hunted animals without a licence is an offence [166] |
| The Kalashnikov | An automatic rifle is a war weapon (سلاح حربي): owning, carrying, buying or selling one is reported as up to 15 years and SAR 150,000 [145]. Hunting with it is also the firearm offence: SAR 80,000 [136], and up to a year and SAR 5,000 under art. 41 (a law firm's article only, uncertain [137]). Whether a semi-automatic AK can be licensed: **not found** |
| Wolves at the herd | Listed as endangered in the Kingdom and protected (80,000 per wolf). Herders' lawful defence is guard dogs, strong pens and herdsmen [149]. No rule was found that allows shooting a wolf attacking a herd |

**The plain answer to the owner's question:** hunting with a Kalashnikov is not legal in
the Kingdom, and owning an automatic one is a serious crime. Lawful hunting is with a
falcon or an air rifle, in season, with a فطري permit, far from farms and towns. Salukis
only in a licensed estate's dog zone.

## 3. What every option keeps

- **Realism first:** the law above applies as it is; every number is sourced or marked.
- **Money is the only progression (GDD 8):** hunting gives no levels or points. Since
  selling game is an offence [166], hunting is **a cost and a pastime, not an income**.
  Falcons are the exception: they are really bought, sold and entered for prizes.
- **The map is the centre (GDD 9):** there is no hunting screen. The player drives the
  Land Cruiser out of the farm onto the real ground. The means of hunting appear as icon
  buttons at the screen's edge while the car is stopped in the open, as the car's own
  buttons do (GDD 9.1). Nothing written floats; a card opens only when tapped.
- **The in-game phone:** permits, licences and the falcon's passport live in phone apps.

## 4. The options

### A. المقناص with a falcon (الصقر)

- **What the player does:**
  1. Buys a falcon: at the breeders' auction at Malham (August), or a locally trapped bird
     at the National Falcon Centre's auction (1 Oct – 30 Nov) [157][158].
  2. Registers it (the falcon's passport) and himself with the Falcons Club, then takes a
     season permit in the phone's فطري app.
  3. Trains it in September and early October (الدعو) [155]. One real day is a game week
     (GDD 2), so training is one short care action **each time the player opens the
     game**, done for all the game days since the last opening (game choice), like the
     herd's care in GDD 3.
  4. In season, drives out with it in the Land Cruiser, stops in open land outside the
     banned areas, looks for tracks, and slips the falcon at a stone-curlew. Houbara only
     in a licensed hunting estate (محازة), a place on the map with a pin.
- **What it earns or costs:** it costs the falcon (farm-bred sakers about SAR 19,000 [158];
  top birds over a million [158]), the permit (fee **not found**: game choice), feed, and
  the trip's fuel and days. It earns nothing directly. A trained falcon is worth more on
  resale (**game choice**: how much training adds).
- **Real:** the season, the permit, the passport, the auctions and their months, the
  quarry by means, the trip in 4x4s with two or three men a car and a camp [155][156].
- **Game choice:** the falcon's skill and how training raises it, the chance a slip
  succeeds, the fees, how a hunt is shown.
- **Weight on the phone (estimate):** one skinned falcon of about 2–3k triangles and 1
  draw. A saker in flight spans about a metre, above the 30 cm rule, so it casts a shadow:
  +1 shadow draw. One or two skinned prey birds of about 2k each. The hunt is away from
  the herd, so it shares the frame with the terrain and the car only: about +5 draws and
  +10k triangles over the drive view.

### B. Air-rifle hunting (الساكتون) in season

- **What the player does:** owns a licensed air rifle (the licence in the phone), takes a
  فطري permit, drives out, stops in the open and taps a flock of doves, sandgrouse or
  quail. One shot takes one bird or misses. **There is no crosshair game:** whether the
  shot hits is decided by chance and the rifle's real reach, within the permit's numbers
  and by day.
- **What it earns or costs:** it costs the rifle (price **not researched**: game choice),
  the licence and the permit. It earns nothing, since game cannot be sold.
- **Real:** air/gas rifles without powder are the only lawful guns [131][132]; one bird a
  shot [127]; species by season [140]. An air rifle has no muzzle flash, so the effect is
  a sound and a puff of dust where the pellet lands.
- **Game choice:** the bag limit per day (the real number was **not found**), the chance
  of a hit, the reach.
- **The alternative, the owner's call:** a real aiming control. It is closer to a shooter
  game (section 6), and it adds a zoomed view with the rifle model in front, about +2
  draws and the rifle's triangles from T7, plus a touch control at the screen's edge.
- **Weight on the phone (estimate):** a small flock as one instanced draw (a few hundred
  triangles a bird), the rifle model from T7 (measured there), a dust puff as one particle
  draw. About +3 draws and +10k triangles.

### C. The Kalashnikov (الكلاش)

The owner asked for the rifle, and the law around it is real. **It puts a black-market war
weapon into a game about a lawful camel owner.** It has no lawful seller, so who sells it
and at what price would both be game choices. There are four ways to place it:

- **C1. Not in the game.** The most realistic for a lawful camel owner. It loses what the
  owner asked for.
- **C2. Allowed, with its risk (as cheating is in GDD 7.1).** The player can buy one from
  an unlawful seller (game choice) and keep it at the farm. It is never a lawful hunting
  tool:
  - Hunting with it brings the firearm fine of SAR 80,000 [136], the Weapons Law [137],
    and the hunting fines for what was shot (wolf 80,000 each [139]).
  - Being found with it (the chance is a game choice, for example a stop by the security
    forces in the open, or at the farm) means the weapon is taken and the real penalty is
    **up to 15 years' prison and SAR 150,000** [145].
  - Firing it at a wedding or celebration is a crime warranting arrest [147].
  - **Prison is the honest trade-off.** The law's penalty is prison. Any replacement (a
    fine and a festival ban, or a pause while the herdsman runs the farm) is a **game
    choice lighter than the law**. It is realism against playability, as GDD 7.1 put
    cheating to the owner. Question 7 asks him.
- **C3. On the wall only.** Kept in the بيت الشعر or the room to look at, never fired.
  Owning it is still the same offence if it is automatic, so C3 is C2 with no use.
- **C4. A licensed rifle instead.** If the owner wants a rifle the player may lawfully
  own, it has to be an individual firearm licensed through Absher (Saudi, 22 or older,
  clean record, toxicology test [146]). Even licensed, it may not be used to hunt [137].
  Whether an AK-pattern semi-automatic can be such a rifle was **not found**.

**Weight on the phone (estimate):** the model is T7's and adds nothing when not in view.
Firing it adds a muzzle flash (one short-lived quad) and a sound.

### D. The herd's safety: wolves and stray dogs (الذئب والكلاب الضالة)

- **What the player does:** nothing new to tap. Calves (حيران) can be lost at night when
  the pens are weak, there is no herdsman, or the herd sleeps out at a grazing camp. The
  defences are the real ones: a strong pen, a herdsman, and a guard dog (a new thing to
  buy) [149].
- **What it earns or costs:** a guard dog's price and feed (**game choice**), and the
  calves lost without one.
- **Real:** wolves enter pens and kill livestock; herders defend with dogs, pens and
  herdsmen; the wolf is protected and killing one costs SAR 80,000 [139][148][149]. Stray
  dog attacks are real in Qassim and elsewhere [151].
- **Game choice:** how often a predator comes, and that it takes calves rather than adult
  camels (wolf attacks on camels were **not found**; the sources speak of sheep and
  goats).
- **Weight on the phone (estimate):** a wolf or dog at night near the pens is one skinned
  animal of about 3–4k triangles. Beside 16 near camels it would be a **17th skinned
  animal**, over the ADR 0005 cap, so during the event the near camels are capped at 15
  (or the budget is raised and measured). It is shown only in an event, so the cost is
  close to zero otherwise.

### E. Falcons as a second contest line (later, if ever)

The falconry festival at Malham has a lure race (الملواح) and a beauty contest (مزاين
الصقور), with prizes reported above SAR 38 million [157], and falcons are a market of their
own [158]. A falcon line could mirror the camel line: buy, train, enter, sell. It is the
only form of hunting that earns money under the real rules, but it is a second game
beside the camels, and the scope is the owner's to decide.

### F. Salukis (السلق)

Hunting hares, jerboas or the dhub with salukis is confirmed only in the dog zones of
licensed fenced estates [142]. It is real but narrow; a saluki is one more skinned animal
of about 4k triangles. It could ride with A as an estate visit, never in open land.

## 5. Where it meets the calendar

The season (1 Sep – 31 Jan) lies over:
- the الوسم rains (from 16 Oct),
- the falcon auctions (Oct–Nov),
- the falconry festival (Nov, or Dec–Jan as in 2025),
- the camel festival (Dec – early Jan),
- mating (from Nov) (GDD 4.4).

A trip costs days that the herd and the festival also want. That trade-off is the
player's, and it is real.

## 6. What it must not become

- Not a shooter: no crosshair game for points, no blood, no kill counts written on screen.
- Not a money farm: game is not sold.
- Not a war on wolves: the wolf is protected and killing one earns no reward.
- Not near the farm: hunting at or around the عزبة is illegal (farms are a banned place),
  so the farm view never becomes a hunting ground.
- Not a source of protected quarry: no oryx, gazelle or ibex hunting, even in estates.
- Not a new permanent overlay: no hunting bar or menu over the map.
